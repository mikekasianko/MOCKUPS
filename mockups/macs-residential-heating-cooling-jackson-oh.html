About Mac's

Built for homeowners who are tired of juggling.

Mac's started as an HVAC company. Over time, we kept seeing the same thing: a homeowner would call us for a furnace, and while we were there, we'd spot an electrical issue or a failing water heater — and they'd have to start over with a new contractor. We got licensed in all three trades so we don't have to leave that behind.

We're based in Jackson, Ohio. We know the neighborhoods, the older homes, and the systems that have been cobbled together over decades. That context matters when you're diagnosing problems across three systems.
