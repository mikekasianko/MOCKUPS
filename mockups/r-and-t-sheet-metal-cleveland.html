About R & T

Sheet metal fabricators who also do HVAC. That combination matters.

Most HVAC contractors order standard parts from a distributor and make them fit. We build the parts — which means the system fits the building, not the other way around.

R & T has been running a sheet metal shop and HVAC operation out of Cleveland for over 25 years. The fabrication side came first — cutting and bending duct components for contractors across Cuyahoga County. The HVAC install and service operation grew out of that, because contractors kept asking us to handle the whole job. Now we do both, and the two sides make each other better. When our installers run into a non-standard situation, they walk back to the shop and build what they need. No waiting. No workarounds.
