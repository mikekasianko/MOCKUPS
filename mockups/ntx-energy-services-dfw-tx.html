How the Tax Credit Works

Real money back from the IRS — not a rebate

Section 25C is a dollar-for-dollar reduction in what you owe the IRS. Here's what that means for common upgrades.

🌡️
Heat Pump

Install a qualifying air-source heat pump and claim up to $2,000 directly off your federal tax bill.

❄️
Central A/C or Furnace

High-efficiency central systems earn up to $600 each — you can combine credits in the same tax year.

🏠
Insulation

Attic insulation and air sealing qualifies for up to $1,200 — often the best ROI upgrade in North Texas.

📋
Stack the Credits

Heat pump + insulation + thermostat in one year can yield $3,000+ total credits. We help you plan the sequence.

We handle the paperwork. When we install a qualifying system, we provide the manufacturer certificate and cost documentation you need for IRS Form 5695. Just hand it to your tax preparer — done.
